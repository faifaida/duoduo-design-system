# Unfinished Experiment Review｜未完成实验复盘卡

## The question｜我原本想验证什么

Write the question without pretending you already knew the answer.

## What happened｜实际发生了什么

Separate evidence from interpretation.

## My interpretation｜我怎么理解

What story am I telling myself about the result?

## Counter-evidence｜反证

What would make that story incomplete or wrong?

## Keep / Change / Stop

- KEEP｜保留
- CHANGE｜修改
- STOP｜停止

## Next test｜下一次测试

Define one smaller test, a time boundary and the evidence that counts.
