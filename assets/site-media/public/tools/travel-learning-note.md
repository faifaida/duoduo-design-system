# Travel Learning Note｜旅行学习记录模板

## Place｜地点

Where am I, and what was the situation?

## Sensory evidence｜感官证据

- What did I see, hear, touch or notice?
- Which object, person or small scene holds the memory?

## Encounter｜相遇

Who did I meet? What did they say in their own language?

## Changed belief｜被修改的判断

- What did I believe before?
- What contradicted it?
- What do I believe now, and how certain am I?

## Transfer｜迁移

How could this change a project, a story, a product or the way I live?

## One follow-up action｜一个后续动作

Turn the note into one interview, one field test or one publishable fragment.
